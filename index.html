Who We Are

A House of Worship & Hope

Eagle Center Ministries is a vibrant family of believers committed to raising people who soar above life's challenges through faith, the Word of God, and the power of the Holy Spirit.

Our Vision

To raise a generation that mounts up with wings like eagles — soaring above every limitation in life. We envision a community transformed by the love of Christ and empowered to reach the world.

Our Mission

To win souls, disciple believers, and equip people to fulfil their God-given purpose. We exist to preach the gospel, nurture spiritual growth, and serve our community with compassion and excellence.

Meet Our Founder

Founder & Apostle

Apostle Mazibuko

Apostle Mazibuko is the visionary founder of Eagle Center Ministries, called to raise a people who rise above life's limitations and mount up on wings like eagles. Through the preaching of God's Word, prayer, and hands-on discipleship, Apostle Mazibuko has shepherded the Eagle Center family with a heart for souls, sound teaching, and community transformation.

Under this leadership, the ministry continues to grow as a home for worship, teaching, and outreach — equipping members to walk in their God-given purpose and carry the gospel into their homes, workplaces, and communities.
